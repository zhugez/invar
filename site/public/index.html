01 / SET UP

Initialize.

Start with a local configuration. Review the commands before running them.

$ invar init
02 / OBSERVE

Capture.

Record exit code, stdout, and stderr. The capture is a candidate, not approval.

$ invar capture
03 / APPROVE

Lock.

Inspect the candidate. Accept its exact hash when you approve the behavior.

$ invar lock --candidate <hash>
04 / COMPARE

Verify.

Change the code. Compare the next run with your accepted examples.

$ invar verify

Less assumption.
More observation.

Give a changing codebase a stable reference. Keep the observations in your repository and the review in your hands.

Exact, by design.

Compare exit code, stdout, and stderr byte for byte. No normalization. No semantic guesswork.

Your machine. Your repo.

Run the CLI locally. Keep configuration and accepted observations under ordinary source review.

Approval is explicit.

Capturing never accepts changes. Verification never rewrites your baseline. You choose what becomes the reference.

Know the boundary.

Configured commands are not sandboxed. Hashes pin a candidate; they don’t authenticate its reviewer.
